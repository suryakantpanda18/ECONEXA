Platform Features

Everything you need for smart waste management

A complete digital ecosystem connecting every stakeholder in the waste management chain.

📊

Real-time Dashboard

Live ward-wise monitoring, collection efficiency tracking, and waste analytics — all in one place for municipal authorities.

📍

Interactive EcoMap

Map all public bins, recycling centers, e-waste facilities, and authorized disposal sites. Filterable, real-time status.

♻️

Waste Education Centre

Comprehensive guides on 9 waste categories — what goes where, how to segregate, and how to dispose responsibly.

🚛

Worker Management

Assign routes, track daily collection targets, monitor household-level status updates from field workers.

📝

Complaint Management

Citizens report missed collections, illegal dumping, overflowing bins. Full workflow from submission to resolution.

🔍

What Goes Where?

Search any waste item — banana peel to old phone — and instantly know the right disposal method and nearest facility.

How It Works

Simple for citizens, powerful for authorities

1

Register & Select Role

Citizens, workers, municipal admins, and state authorities each get a personalized experience.

2

Log & Monitor Waste

Citizens log daily waste. Workers track collections. Admins see it all aggregated in real-time.

3

Manage & Resolve

Report issues, assign workers, track complaints to resolution. Close the loop digitally.

4

Analyze & Improve

Rich analytics at ward, municipality and state level drive data-driven waste management decisions.
